    Did it find what was planted?

    The log was written with these events. A claim finds one when its citation holds, it cites a key line, and it uses one of the event's words.

    What the checker tests, and what it cannot

    A passing claim has real evidence behind its details. It is not proof that the claim reads that evidence correctly: “this is a brute-force attack” cites the right lines whether or not the conclusion is sound. Judgement stays with the analyst.
